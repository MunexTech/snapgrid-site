Snapgrid

Block Puzzle

Drag, snap, clear. A colorful, satisfying block puzzle you can play with one hand: no timers, no pressure, no account.

Get it on Google Play
Free to play, supported by ads. Android 7.0 and newer.

Fair deals

Every tray is checked against your board before it is dealt, so at least one piece always fits. Nothing adapts to how well you are doing.

Undo

Made a slip? Take back your last move. Your first undo each game is free.

Daily Challenge

Everyone gets the same pieces each day. Come back every day to build a streak.

High score

Your best score is always on screen, with a meter that fills as you close in on it.

Four themes

Start in Lagoon, then unlock Dusk, Daylight and Ember as you improve.

Plays offline

No sign-in and no account. Only a small banner under the board while you play.
